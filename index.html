Who Is This Judah Guy?

Hi! I’m Judah Owens, and this is my website/blog! I am an undergraduate student at the University of Michigan (my current degree path is neuroscience). I love being outdoors, cooking, technology, and philosophy.

I’ve been wanting to do some sort of blogging for a while, and I had a spare afternoon and didn’t want to do homework, so I made this website. I’m going to try to write some sort of post once a month. What will that post be about? I have no clue; I’m just going to write about whatever I am interested in at the moment.
